About Us

At University Rocketry Team, we design, manufacture, and launch high-power sounding rockets to a world-class standard.

The Team

We are a highly technical team of student engineers who design, manufacture and test high-power sounding rockets to compete in domestic and international competitions.

Our Vision

Our passionate team strives to innovate and push the boundaries of the sounding rocket environment every day, and we hope to inspire the next generation of student engineers.

Our Rockets

Our rockets are designed for performance and built with integrity. We take a systems engineering approach, ensuring meticulous crafting and rigorous testing.

Our Sponsors

We would not have been able to achieve our major milestones without the generous support of our sponsors, making all our rocketry projects possible.
